## How I Work

I do my best work independently, with room to think a problem through before acting on it. I like to have the theory solid before I start building — if something doesn't make sense yet, I'll pause and dig into why instead of moving forward on a guess.

I ask for help when I actually need it rather than by default — but I put just as much time into the other side of that: I spend a good amount of my time mentoring newer and junior teammates, walking them through problems and sharing what I've picked up.

I'm INTJ, Enneagram 6w5. In practice, that tends to show up as planning before committing to an approach, and leaning on structure and logic when making decisions. I'm also actively working on strengthening my communication and collaboration skills, treating it as a skill to build deliberately, the same way I'd approach learning a new stack.
